Edyta Śliwińska

Edyta is a world-renowned ballroom dancer and a professional from the hit television series Dancing with the Stars. If you watched the early seasons, you already know her — the Polish-born pro who made first-time celebrity dancers look like they belonged on that floor.

Her career was built on teaching people who had never danced. That is why couples and teams book her: she breaks complex movement into steps anyone can feel, then adds the polish that made her a fan favorite on national TV.

Today she works two tracks from her San Rafael studio: wedding first dances for couples, planners, and venues, and corporate events for teams who want a shared win instead of another icebreaker.

Live stage & production

Edyta has also executive produced and choreographed live stage shows — competition formats with audience voting, narrative-driven productions, and world-class dance showcases.
