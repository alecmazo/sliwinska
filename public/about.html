Edyta Śliwińska

Edyta is a world-renowned ballroom dancer, instructor, and professional from the hit television series Dancing with the Stars. With a career built on teaching celebrities who had never danced before, she breaks complex movement into steps anyone can feel.

Whether leading an energetic intro session or a sophisticated masterclass, she delivers a professional, memorable experience that motivates people and builds real connection.

Live stage & production

Edyta has executive produced and choreographed live stage shows — competition formats with audience voting, narrative-driven productions, and world-class dance showcases.
