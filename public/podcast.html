About the show

The Danceology podcast interviews the great masters of dance, the working professionals starring in today’s most exciting shows, and the rising stars ready to amp up the craft. Join host Edyta Śliwińska from Dancing with the Stars as she dives into the world of dance — unique stories and fascinating personalities.
